You were most likely sent this link because a track was flagged as possibly AI-generated, and the musician wants to show it is not. This page is their evidence, laid out for you to read in a couple of minutes.

## What the page contains

Reading top to bottom, you will see:

- **A verdict.** A plain-language headline (for example, "Strong evidence of real studio production") with a colored band. This is SessionSeal's summary of how much real-studio-work evidence the record holds.
- **A sealed line.** Confirmation that the record was cryptographically sealed on a specific date and has not changed since, with a link to the signed record itself.
- **The material.** Where the musician allowed it, you can listen to the isolated stems (the separate layers of the song) and sometimes download the full project session. This is the most direct thing you can check yourself.
- **The evidence.** A short checklist of the signals that point to real studio work, each with a sentence on why it matters.
- **The technical detail.** A collapsible section with the exact checks, results, and scores, for reviewers who want to audit the reasoning.

## What it is claiming

The page is careful about this: it is evidence that **real studio work went into the track**, the kind of work an AI generator does not produce. It is not, on its own, a claim about who wrote the song. See [Someone flagged this track as AI. Does this page prove a human made it?](/faq/does-this-prove-a-human-made-it) for exactly where that line sits.

## You do not need an account

The page is self-serve. If you want to hear the stems or download the session, you enter your email so the musician knows who reviewed the material, but there is nothing to sign up for.
